The White House is asking OpenAI to slow roll the release of its new model over safety concerns

OpenAI is slowing the release of its new GPT 5.6 model due to safety concerns from the White House. The limited release is expected to be followed by a broader release a couple of weeks later, depending on the outcome. The White House, specifically the Office of the National Cyber Director and the Office of Science and Technology Policy, is reviewing the model and worked closely with OpenAI on the release. The move is similar to Anthropic's decision to limit the release of its Claude Mythos model, citing concerns over potential misuse by cybercriminals. The concern is that powerful AI models can identify and exploit software vulnerabilities at high speeds, posing a significant threat to organizations with complex software infrastructure. The White House's involvement may impact the development and release of future AI models, with potential implications for the industry's growth and regulation. The limited release of GPT 5.6 may also affect OpenAI's competitive positioning, as other companies may be subject to similar oversight and restrictions.
