Ahead of its IPO, Anthropic’s Daniela Amodei shrugs off doubts about AI’s returns

Anthropic filed confidentially for an IPO, following a $65bn fundraise at a $965bn valuation, with co-founder Daniela Amodei citing capital needs to train and serve AI models. Annualized revenue rose to $47bn in May, up from c.$9bn at the end of 2025, driven by AI model demand. Amodei expects businesses to continue deploying AI, driving efficiency and creativity in sectors like coding, financial services, and healthcare. The company partnered with xAI for compute capacity at a cost of $1.25bn per month, opting not to build its own data centers to avoid overextending compute resources. Anthropic's IPO filing and rapid growth may alleviate concerns about AI returns, with the company's valuation implying a price-to-sales ratio of c.20.6, versus peers like Nvidia at c.24.3.
