The company's restrictions on ads appear in a dedicated section of its new Apple Advertising Services policy, which details rules for publishing ads across Apple's first-party apps. Apple's approach to Maps ads may expand to other categories over time, but its initial focus is on a more curated, navigation-focused product. The company's decision to only show a single ad to users in its Maps search results differs from Google's approach, and may help Apple avoid some of the verification and audit issues associated with home services businesses.
