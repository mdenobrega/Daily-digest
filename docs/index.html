Rocket Lab continues buying spree by acquiring satellite company Iridium

Rocket Lab is acquiring Iridium for USD8bn, valuing the satellite company at USD8bn. The deal will see Rocket Lab acquire Iridium's stock for USD54 per share. Rocket Lab plans to build upon Iridium's existing network to scale into new markets. Iridium operates 66 satellites and has 2.5m subscribers, with a highly profitable business. Rocket Lab's shares rose on the news, as the company expands its space services. The acquisition is part of Rocket Lab's buying spree, following deals for Motiv, Mynaric, and Geost.

Rocket Lab aims to challenge SpaceX with the acquisition, leveraging Iridium's valuable spectrum. The deal mirrors SpaceX's Starlink push, with Rocket Lab planning to deploy next-gen satellites. Iridium's constellation provides communications for 2.5m subscribers, with a strong government customer base. Rocket Lab will help deploy Iridium's next-gen satellites, including direct-to-device services for US national security. The acquisition puts Rocket Lab in a better position to compete with SpaceX, with a more comprehensive space services offering. The deal is part of a wave of consolidation in the industry, with Amazon buying Globalstar for USD11.6bn in April 2026.
